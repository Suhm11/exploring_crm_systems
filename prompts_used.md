Part 5: AI Reflection and Prompt Engineering

Prompt 1

What is a CRM system? Explain its definition, purpose, history, and evolution in simple terms.

Why it was useful

This prompt helped me learn the basics of CRM systems. It gave me a simple explanation and helped me get started with my research.

Prompt 2

Why do organizations use CRM systems? Explain sales management, customer management, marketing, customer support, and reporting.

Why it was useful

This prompt helped me understand why businesses use CRM systems and how they help different departments.

Prompt 3

Compare Salesforce, HubSpot CRM, Zoho CRM, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM. Include strengths, weaknesses, target customers, and pricing models.

Why it was useful

This prompt helped me compare different CRM products and learn the advantages and disadvantages of each one.

Prompt 4

Compare SuiteCRM, EspoCRM, and Odoo CRM. Include features, technology stack, community support, and ease of installation.

Why it was useful

This prompt helped me learn about open-source CRM systems and understand how they are different from commercial CRM products.

Prompt 5

Assume you are designing a CRM system using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery, Bootstrap, PHP, and MySQL. What modules, database tables, libraries, and security features would be needed?

Why it was useful

This prompt helped me understand how a CRM system could be built and what technologies would be needed.

AI Effectiveness Analysis

What AI did well?

AI did a good job explaining CRM topics and comparing different CRM systems. It helped me find information quickly and made it easier to learn about a topic that was new to me.

What AI struggled with?

Sometimes the answers were longer than necessary and included extra information that I did not need for the assignment. I had to shorten some of the responses.

What information required validation?

Information about software features, pricing, and product details needed to be checked because these things can change over time.

What surprised you?

I was surprised by how quickly AI could explain CRM systems and compare different products. It saved a lot of time during research.

What would you do differently next time?

Next time I would ask more detailed questions from the beginning so I could get more focused answers.

Would you trust AI for software research?

I would use AI as a starting point for software research, but I would still check important information using other sources. AI is helpful for learning and research, but information should always be verified.
