### 1. What is CRM?

#### Definition

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. It is a software that businesses use to keep track of customer information and interactions. A CRM system stores things like contact information, communication history, and sales activity in one place.

#### Purpose

The main purpose of a CRM system is to help businesses manage customer relationships more effectively. It helps employees stay organized by keeping customer data, sales information, marketing activities, and support requests together in one system.

#### History

CRM systems became more common in the 1980s when businesses started using computers and databases to store customer information. During the 1990s, companies began using dedicated CRM software to better manage customer relationships and sales activities.

#### Evolution

Over the years, CRM systems have become much more advanced. Instead of only storing customer information, modern CRM platforms can automate tasks, create reports, track sales performance, and connect with other business software. Many CRM systems are now cloud-based, making them easier to access from different locations.
